## barbarian.name
Barbarian

## barbarian.feature.rage.name
Rage

## barbarian.feature.rage.desc
You can enter a rage as a Bonus Action if you aren't wearing Heavy armor.

While active, you gain Advantage on Strength checks and Strength saving throws, a bonus to damage rolls with Strength-based melee attacks, and Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage.

You can use this feature twice. You regain one expended use when you finish a Short Rest, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## barbarian.feature.unarmored-defense.name
Unarmored Defense

## barbarian.feature.unarmored-defense.desc

## barbarian.feature.weapon-mastery.desc
Your training with weapons allows you to use the mastery properties of two kinds of Simple or Martial Melee weapons of your choice, such as Greataxes and Handaxes.

Whenever you finish a Long Rest, you can change the kinds of weapons you chose.

## barbarian.feature.danger-sense.desc
You gain an uncanny sense of when things aren't as they should be, giving you an edge when you dodge perils. You have Advantage on Dexterity saving throws unless you have the Incapacitated condition.

## barbarian.feature.reckless-attack.name

## barbarian.feature.reckless-attack.desc
When you make your first attack roll on your turn, you can decide to attack recklessly. Doing so gives you Advantage on attack rolls using Strength until the start of your next turn, but attack rolls against you have Advantage during that time.

Bardic Inspiration

## bard.feature.bardic-inspiration.desc
You can inspire others through words or music. As a Bonus Action, you can give one creature other than yourself within 60 feet of you a Bardic Inspiration die (a d6).

Once within the next hour, the creature can roll the die and add the number rolled to one ability check, attack roll, or saving throw it makes. The creature can wait until after rolling the d20 before deciding to use the Bardic Inspiration die.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of once), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

The die becomes a d8 when you reach level 5.

## druid.subclass.circle-of-the-moon.desc
Druids of the Circle of the Moon draw on lunar magic to transform themselves. Their order gathers under the moon to share news and perform rituals. Changeable as the moon, a Druid of this circle might prowl as a great cat one night, soar over the treetops as an eagle the next day, and then crash through undergrowth as a bear to drive off a trespassing monster.

## druid.subclass.circle-of-the-moon.feature.circle-of-the-moon-spells.name
Circle of the Moon Spells

## druid.subclass.circle-of-the-moon.feature.circle-of-the-moon-spells.desc
The magic of your circle ensures you always have Cure Wounds, Moonbeam, and Starry Wisp prepared. You can cast these spells while in a Wild Shape form.

## druid.subclass.circle-of-the-moon.feature.circle-forms.name
Circle Forms

## druid.subclass.circle-of-the-moon.feature.circle-forms.desc
You can channel lunar magic when you assume a Wild Shape form. The maximum Challenge Rating for the form equals your Druid level divided by 3 (round down). Until you leave the form, your AC equals 13 plus your Wisdom modifier if that total is higher than the Beast's AC. You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to three times your Druid level when you enter the form.

## druid.subclass.circle-of-the-sea.name
Circle of the Sea

## druid.subclass.circle-of-the-sea.desc
Druids of the Circle of the Sea draw on the tempestuous forces of oceans and storms. Some view themselves as embodiments of nature's wrath, seeking vengeance against those who despoil nature. Others seek mystical unity with nature by attuning themselves to the ebb and flow of the tides.

## druid.subclass.circle-of-the-sea.feature.circle-of-the-sea-spells.name
Circle of the Sea Spells

## druid.subclass.circle-of-the-sea.feature.circle-of-the-sea-spells.desc
The magic of your circle ensures you always have Fog Cloud, Gust of Wind, Ray of Frost, Shatter, and Thunderwave prepared.

## druid.subclass.circle-of-the-sea.feature.wrath-of-the-sea.name
Wrath of the Sea

## druid.subclass.circle-of-the-sea.feature.wrath-of-the-sea.desc
As a Bonus Action, you can expend a use of your Wild Shape to manifest a 5-foot Emanation of ocean spray that surrounds you for 10 minutes. When you manifest the Emanation and as a Bonus Action on your subsequent turns, you can choose another creature you can see in the Emanation. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC or take Cold damage and, if the creature is Large or smaller, be pushed up to 15 feet away from you. To determine this damage, roll a number of d6s equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of one die).

## druid.subclass.circle-of-stars.name
Circle of the Stars

## druid.subclass.circle-of-stars.desc
The Circle of the Stars has tracked heavenly patterns since time immemorial, discovering secrets hidden amid the constellations. By understanding these secrets, the Druids of this circle seek to harness the powers of the cosmos.

## druid.subclass.circle-of-stars.feature.star-map.name
Star Map

## druid.subclass.circle-of-stars.feature.star-map.desc
You've created a star chart as part of your heavenly studies. It is a Tiny object, and you can use it as a Spellcasting Focus for your Druid spells. You determine its form by rolling on the Star Map table or by choosing one. While holding the map, you have the Guidance and Guiding Bolt spells prepared, and you can cast Guiding Bolt without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once), regaining all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

## druid.subclass.circle-of-stars.feature.starry-form.name
Starry Form

## druid.subclass.circle-of-stars.feature.starry-form.desc
As a Bonus Action, you can expend a use of your Wild Shape feature to take on a starry form rather than shape-shifting. While in your starry form, you retain your game statistics, but your body becomes luminous. The form lasts for 10 minutes. Whenever you assume your starry form, choose which constellation glimmers on your body.

## fighter.subclass.psi-warrior.feature.telekinetic-movement.name

## fighter.subclass.psi-warrior.feature.telekinetic-movement.desc

Once you take this action, you can't do so again until you finish a Short Rest or Long Rest unless you expend a Psionic Energy Die (no action required) to restore your use of it.
